This policy explains what data Duda Studio ("we", "the Service"), operated by Alexandre Melo, Brazil, processes when you use it, and why. The controller for this data is Alexandre Melo, contactable at alexandre.carvalho.melo@gmail.com.

1. Data we process

  • TikTok basic profile (scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar. Used only to show you which account you are signed in with.
  • TikTok creator settings returned by TikTok before each post: nickname, username, avatar, the privacy levels your account allows, whether comments/Duet/Stitch are turned off, and your maximum video length. Used only to build the posting page and validate your post.
  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) issued by TikTok after you authorize the app. Used only to call TikTok's API on your behalf when you post.
  • Content you choose to post: the video file, title and post settings. Sent to TikTok only after you press Post (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • Post status (publish ID and processing status), used to show you whether the post succeeded.

We do not request or receive your TikTok password, your followers, your messages, or your viewing history.

2. How data is stored

  • Tokens are stored on the Service's server only, in access-restricted storage, and are never shown in the browser or shared.
  • Video files are stored temporarily only while they are being transferred to TikTok and are deleted immediately after the transfer finishes or fails.
  • This information website is static: it uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. The OAuth callback page only passes the authorization result to the Duda Studio app in your own browser.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties and do not use it for advertising. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as needed to sign you in and publish the content you choose.

4. Legal basis

We process this data to provide the Service you request (performance of a contract) and with your consent given on TikTok's authorization screen, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Lei nº 13.709/2018) and other applicable laws.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while you remain signed in. When you log out, Duda Studio revokes its access token with TikTok and deletes your stored tokens and profile data. TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours and refresh tokens after 365 days. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services / Manage app permissions).

6. Your rights

You may request confirmation, access, correction, portability or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing alexandre.carvalho.melo@gmail.com. We will respond within the time limits set by law.

7. Security

Communication with TikTok uses HTTPS. App credentials and tokens are kept secret and stored with restricted file permissions.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age for TikTok in your country) and we do not knowingly process their data.

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
