What is Duda Studio?

Duda Studio is a web app for content creators who produce short videos on their computer and want to publish them to their own TikTok account in a few clicks. You sign in with TikTok, pick a video you made, review it in a preview, choose your caption and post settings, and publish it directly to your profile with TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Features

  • Sign in with TikTok – secure OAuth login; Duda Studio never sees your TikTok password.
  • Know where you post – your TikTok nickname and avatar are shown on the posting page, so you always know which account the video goes to.
  • Full control of every post – you write the title, choose who can view the video (from the options your account allows), and decide whether people can comment, Duet or Stitch. Nothing is pre-selected for you.
  • Honest labels – built-in commercial content disclosure ("Your brand" / "Branded content") and an AI-generated content label, using TikTok's official labels.
  • Preview before posting – watch your video before it is sent; nothing is uploaded until you press Post.
  • Post status – follow the upload and publishing progress until your video is live.
  • No watermarks – Duda Studio never adds logos, watermarks or promotional text to your content.

How it works

  1. Click Continue with TikTok and approve the permissions: basic profile (user.info.basic), upload (video.upload) and publish (video.publish).
  2. Duda Studio loads your latest creator settings from TikTok (nickname, avatar, allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length).
  3. Select a video from your computer, watch the preview, add a title, choose the privacy level and interaction settings, and disclose commercial content if applicable.
  4. Press Post. The video is sent to TikTok and published to your profile. It may take a few minutes to process before it appears.

Who is it for?

Creators who publish their own original content. You can only post to the TikTok account you signed in with, and only content you created or have the rights to publish. Duda Studio does not copy content from other platforms or other accounts.
